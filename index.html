💡 Study Tips

Everything you need to study smarter, not harder.

📚 Preparation Strategies

🌱

Early Preparation

Start at least 2–3 weeks before an exam. This gives your brain time to move information from short-term to long-term memory.

  • Review notes within 24 hours of each class
  • Create a study schedule and stick to it
  • Break subjects into small daily chunks
  • Use active recall instead of re-reading
⏰

Late Preparation

Short on time? Focus on high-yield material first. Prioritize what's most likely to appear on the exam.

  • Identify key topics from past papers
  • Use the Pomodoro technique (25 min focus, 5 min break)
  • Focus on understanding, not memorizing
  • Get at least 6 hours of sleep — it helps memory

🍎 Food & Brain Fuel

🐟

Brain-Boosting Foods

What you eat directly affects focus, memory, and energy levels.

  • Fatty fish (salmon, sardines) — rich in Omega-3
  • Blueberries — antioxidants for memory
  • Dark chocolate — boosts focus & mood
  • Nuts & seeds — vitamin E for brain health
  • Whole grains — steady energy release
  • Eggs — choline for memory retention
💧

Hydration & Snacks

Even mild dehydration reduces concentration and memory by up to 10%.

  • Drink 2–3 litres of water daily
  • Green tea — caffeine + L-theanine for calm focus
  • Avoid sugary drinks — they cause energy crashes
  • Snack on fruit, yogurt, or trail mix
  • Limit coffee to 1–2 cups per study session
🚫

Foods to Avoid

Some foods slow you down and make it harder to concentrate.

  • Heavy fried foods — cause sluggishness
  • Energy drinks — crash after 2 hours
  • Excess sugar — brain fog & fatigue
  • Alcohol — disrupts memory consolidation

🧠 Memory Techniques

🔁

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day → 3 days → 1 week → 2 weeks → 1 month. This beats cramming every time.

🃏

Active Recall

Close the book and try to write down everything you remember. Then check. This forces your brain to retrieve information, strengthening memory.

🏛️

Method of Loci

Imagine walking through a familiar place. Place items you need to remember at specific spots along the route. Walk through it mentally to recall.

📖

Storytelling

Turn facts into a story. Our brains remember narratives 22× better than isolated facts.

🗣️

Teach It

Explain the topic out loud as if teaching someone else. Gaps in your understanding become obvious instantly.

🎵

Mnemonics

Use acronyms, rhymes, or songs. e.g. "My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles" for the planets.

✍️

Handwriting

Writing by hand engages more brain regions than typing and improves retention by up to 30%.

😴

Sleep & Memory

During deep sleep, your brain replays what you learned and stores it long-term. 7–8 hours is non-negotiable.

⚡ Focus & Productivity

🍅

Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 min, break for 5 min. After 4 rounds, take a 20-min break. Prevents burnout and keeps focus sharp.

📱

Eliminate Distractions

Put your phone in another room. Even a silent phone on your desk reduces focus by 20%.

🎯

Set Clear Goals

Instead of "study biology," say "understand cell division and draw it from memory." Specific = effective.

🌅

Study in the Morning

Your brain is freshest 1–2 hours after waking. Use this window for the hardest subjects.

😌 Stress & Wellbeing

🧘

Manage Stress

Take 5 deep breaths before each study session. Stress blocks memory retrieval — calm minds learn faster.

🚶

Move Your Body

A 10-minute walk before studying increases blood flow to the brain and boosts focus for up to 2 hours.

🎯

Celebrate Small Wins

Reward yourself after each session. Positive reinforcement keeps motivation high over long study periods.

🤝

Study with Others

Group study works when done right — explain concepts to each other, quiz each other, stay on topic.

📝 Exam Day Tips

🌙

Night Before

  • No cramming — review light notes only
  • Prepare everything (pen, calculator, ID)
  • Sleep 7–8 hours minimum
  • Set two alarms
🌅

Morning Of

  • Eat a protein-rich breakfast
  • Arrive 15 minutes early
  • Avoid panicked last-minute cramming
  • Take 5 deep breaths before starting
✍️

During the Exam

  • Read all questions first
  • Start with what you know best
  • Manage time — don't get stuck
  • Leave 10 minutes to review
